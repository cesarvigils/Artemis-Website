# Artemis brand core, as applied to the website

This is the merged, applied brand core for `artemisesports.com`. It is not a
new document: sections 1 to 3 and 5 to 8 are reproduced verbatim from
**Artemis Esports — Brand Core** (the iRacing-scoped version, which governs
naming today), followed by that document's growth triggers, then an appendix
holding the parts of **Brand Core v2** that only become live once a trigger
fires, then a table saying where each rule lives in this repository.

Two sections of the source document are deliberately not reproduced here
because they are about how the org talks to itself rather than about the
website: section 4 (who reads what) and section 10 (governance). The source
file is the authority for both.

When a rule in this document and a generic design rule disagree, this document
wins, and `DESIGN.md` records the conflict and the choice.

---

## 1. Positioning

**For** iRacing drivers who want structure, seat time, and a record of what
they've done, **Artemis is** a small endurance team that develops in the open
and puts every result on the record — **unlike** teams that recruit loud and go
quiet.

Small is a fact, not a flaw. It's also what makes "in the open" possible: five
people can actually do it.

**What we are not:** a content house, a lifestyle brand, or a multi-game org.
Not yet.

---

## 2. Mission, promise, motto

**Mission.** Artemis fields an iRacing endurance crew, develops drivers who
don't have a team yet, and publishes what we achieve.

**Promise.** *Every shot on the record.*

**Motto.** *Take the shot.*

The promise is the accountability. The motto is the action — the overtake, the
committed stint, the call from the pit wall. Both hold when the org is bigger
and in other games, so they don't change later.

---

## 3. What we stand for

**Publish the result.** Every race we enter as a team goes public within 48
hours, win or loss. *Test: can a stranger find our last five results without
asking?*

**Debrief in the open.** Every race gets a short written debrief and the
Garage 61 data is shared. *Test: does the crew have a doc of what it's fixing
before the next round?*

**The driver is the point.** Time and money go to seat time and coaching before
merch and graphics. *Test: name the last thing we did for a driver that nobody
saw.*

---

## 5. Naming

**The team is "Artemis."** One roster means the org name is the team name.
"Artemis iRacing" only exists once there's a second roster to distinguish it
from.

**The Scoreboard** — the result post. After every team race, not on a fixed
weekly schedule if there's nothing to report. Fake cadence is worse than honest
silence.

**Orion** — the Discord bot. The one myth reference we've earned. No others
until a second tool genuinely needs a name.

**Roles** — internally, whatever the Discord already has. Externally, there's
the owner and the crew. Don't publish an org chart for five people.

**No fan name, no academy, no content series beyond the Scoreboard.** Each of
those is a promise to fill a structure. See the growth triggers for when they
get added.

## 6. Visual system

This section is size-agnostic. It's what makes a five-person team look like a
team, and it doesn't change when the team grows.

### Colors

| Hex | Name | Job | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#000A08` | Night | The field. | ~80% |
| `#0A1714` `#12211D` `#1E322C` | Surface ramp | Panels and dividers so the black has depth. | as needed |
| `#486B62` `#8FA8A1` | Muted text | Captions, metadata. | as needed |
| `#EBFFFB` | Mist | Type and UI on dark. | most text |
| `#0E7F6B` | Deep Teal | Brand color when it needs area — livery panels, gradients. | when needed |
| `#0FFFCF` | **Signal** | The one thing the eye lands on. The A, the Arc, one accent per layout. | **5–10%** |

Signal is dark-mode only; it fails contrast on Mist and passes on Night. Never
fills a shape bigger than a line or a small block. No graffiti, no splash, no
scrawled words. Texture is material — grain, carbon, brushed metal at 3–5% —
never vandalism.

### Logo system

**Wordmark** — the workhorse. Holds to 100px wide. Headers, overlays, jersey,
templates. Don't alter.

**Icon** — the notched A, isolated. Discord icon, avatar, favicon, Twitch
panels, stream bug, watermark. One color. Most people will see this most often.

**Hero mark** — the profile. 96px and above only: banners, the 1:1 wallpaper,
jersey chest, livery hero panel. Never an avatar.

Ask the designer for a 1-color A icon and a minimum-size spec if they're not
already in the package.

### The Arc

One recurring shape, same weight every time, in Signal: a crescent whose inner
edge sharpens into an arrowhead. Derived from the geometry of the notched A.
Livery sidepod, jersey collar, overlay corner, social post frame. It replaces
the five scrawled "HUNT"s.

### Type

Display: **Archivo** or **Oswald**. Body, UI, numbers: **Inter** (tabular
numerals for lap times). Semi-bold minimum on stream. Retire Nexa and Eastman.

### Grid

Lockup at 8% from the left edge, vertically centered, on every asset. Same
margin, every post.

## 7. Voice

Like a driver on the radio right after a stint: plain, specific, a little dry.
Lead with the fact and stop. Racing language used plainly. No trash talk, no
exclamation spam, and never a sentence about how quiet we are.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "P4 at Suzuka 1000. Full result below." | "We build in silence and compete with precision." |
| "Swapped in P7, out in P4. The crew did that." | "Results speak louder than the noise." |
| "Rough one. Lost time in the stops." | "Heartbreaking result but we'll come back stronger 💔🔥" |
| "[Driver] joins for Petit Le Mans." | "We are BEYOND excited to announce…" |
| "Reps paid off." | "The grind never stops." |
| "GG [team]." | "Called by the hunt." |

**Templates.**
Win — `[Result] — [event]. [One factual detail]. Recap: [link].`
Loss — `[Result] — [event]. [What went wrong, plainly]. [What we're fixing].`
Roster — `[Handle] joins the crew. [Role]. Welcome.`
Partner — `Artemis x [Partner]. [What it does for drivers, one line].` Owner
approval required.

## 8. Proof points

**True now:** community since 2017; league results including a first career win
and a P4 at the Suzuka 1000; live Garage 61 telemetry; a professionally
designed identity.

**True once the cadence runs:** a public results record; verifiable posting
frequency; Metricool follower and view data.

**Not true — don't claim:** audience size, partner track record, multiple
titles.

## When this document grows

Each line is a trigger. Nothing here is live until its trigger fires.

- **Second title has a full roster and someone coaching it** → rosters become
  "Artemis [Game]"; the org name becomes the umbrella. Never open a division to
  look bigger.
- **Ten or more active competing members** → consider a development roster,
  called "Artemis Academy." Plain on purpose.
- **Members start calling themselves something on their own** → adopt it. Don't
  invent one first.
- **A second content stream has three months of material behind it** → give it
  a name. Not before.
- **First cash partner** → form the LLC; the partner template goes live.
- **Any roster starts winning consistently** → it leads the brand, whatever
  game it's in.

When a trigger fires, the voice, visual system, promise, and motto don't
change. Only the naming layer and the per-title tone dials get added.

## Appendix: from v2, applies when triggers fire

Nothing in this appendix is live on the website today. It is reproduced so that
whoever applies a trigger does not have to go looking for a second file.

### Naming system

| Thing | Name | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Game rosters | **Artemis [Game]** — Artemis iRacing, Artemis Valorant, Artemis Rocket League, Artemis CS2, Artemis Apex, Artemis Call of Duty | Never themed per roster. Entering or leaving a title costs zero rebrand. |
| Development roster | **Artemis Academy** | Plain on purpose. Legible to sponsors and recruits instantly. |
| Fan community | **the Range** | *Offered, not imposed.* Use "Artemis fans" until members use "the Range" on their own. |
| Results series | **The Scoreboard** | Weekly. Every result, every roster. |
| Behind-the-scenes series | **Off the Clock** | People, not highlights. |
| Development series | **Reps** | Coaching, review, the work. |
| Staff tiers | **Staff / Leadership / Director** | Keep. Do not rename into Greek. |
| Discord bot | **Orion** | Keep — the one earned myth reference. A second tool, if ever needed: **Leto** or **Delos**. Then stop. |
| Recurring graphic device | **the Arc** | See the visual system. |

**Naming rules.** Don't theme channels. Don't give roles epithets. Don't name
rosters after creatures. Every themed name is something a new member has to
learn before they can participate. The myth is a design language, not a
vocabulary test.

### Per-title flex

The dials move; the voice doesn't.

- **iRacing** — most matter-of-fact. Stint, delta, pit language. This roster
  leads the brand until another one wins more.
- **CS2** — measured. The oldest scene rewards restraint.
- **Rocket League** — most playful. Funny and enthusiastic allowed.
- **Apex** — clip-driven. Scoreboard and highlights; keep teal minimal so
  footage reads.
- **Call of Duty** — results-forward. No military-adjacent phrasing under any
  circumstances.
- **Valorant** — round and clutch language. Roster graphics identical to every
  other title.

Note on the naming table against today's site: the site says **Artemis** for
the team and **Artemis Esports** only where the org itself is named (the
wordmark, the `<title>`, the structured data, the copyright line). That is the
section 5 rule, not a v2 one; when the second-roster trigger fires, "Artemis
iRacing" replaces "Artemis" in `src/data/site.json` and nowhere else.
